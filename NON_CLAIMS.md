# Non-Claims

1. We do not claim a proof of the global Masur–Veech spectrum.
2. Individual exponents on the even component of $\mathcal{H}(6)$ are due to Yu–Zuo.
3. Prym containment in the even component is due to Möller.
4. The 8-square flatsurf table is a consistency check, not a Prym-filtered spectrum.
5. `promote_ready = false`.

The Lean uniqueness lemma (`unique_scaled_pair`; see [docs/UNIQUENESS_LEMMA.md](docs/UNIQUENESS_LEMMA.md)) certifies only that a $2:1$ positive ratio and the EKZ sum hypothesis $6/7$ determine $4/7$ and $2/7$. It is not a proof of the global spectrum.
